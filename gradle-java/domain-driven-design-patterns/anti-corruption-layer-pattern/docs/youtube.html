Description

The first two lines are what a viewer sees above the fold, so they carry the hook rather than the boilerplate.

Anti-Corruption Layer pattern in Java: the layer is a translator between your model and theirs, so the other system's names and codes never leak into yours, like an interpreter at a business meeting. Explained with an online store that must talk to an old inventory system nobody is allowed to change. We watch the old system's codes spread through four features, then translate them once in one place. We hear bad data stopped at the door, the other side change without touching our model, and what the layer costs and protects. The price is a translator you must maintain.
