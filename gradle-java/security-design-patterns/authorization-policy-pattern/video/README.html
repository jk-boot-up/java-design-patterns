Suggested description:

Authorization Policy pattern in Java: signing in proves who you are; authorization decides what you may do, and a policy makes every one of those decisions in one place, from rules about roles and about details such as who owns what, refusing anything no rule allows, like a hotel key-card system. Explained with an online store that has customers, support staff and an admin. We watch checks scattered through every endpoint, see roles alone fall short, add rules on attributes, and deny by default. We finish with the bill: one place decides, and the answer starts as no.
