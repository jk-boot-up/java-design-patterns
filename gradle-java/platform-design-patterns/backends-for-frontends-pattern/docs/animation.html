Fewer bytes is the obvious win. Who is allowed to change the shape is the real one.
A field selector fixes the size completely and fixes nothing that matters. And the three costs at the end — one business rule copied into two backends, the shared jobs done once per backend, and a backend per client until there is a department — are the price of the pattern, not mistakes made while applying it.
