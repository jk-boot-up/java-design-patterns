The structure is Decorator. The decision is about deployment.
Everything this pattern gives you — a policy changed without opening a service, in a language your library does not have to support — and everything it charges you — a second process per service, a second thing that can be down, a millisecond on every call — comes from one fact: the proxy runs beside the service rather than inside it.
