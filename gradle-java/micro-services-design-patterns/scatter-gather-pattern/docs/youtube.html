Description

The first two lines are what a viewer sees above the fold, so they carry the hook rather than the boilerplate.

Scatter-Gather pattern in Java: Scatter-gather sends one request to many parties at the same time, gathers their answers up to a deadline, and combines them into one. Explained with an online store product page that shows the best price from four suppliers. We ask the four suppliers one after another, then all at once, stop the slowest one from setting the pace with a deadline, make the page say honestly what was left out, and keep one supplier's failure from failing the page. The bill is that one view becomes many calls.
